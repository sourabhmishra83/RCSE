# Full-population selective execution study

The latest [value-calibration mechanism check](../revision_complete/value_calibration_extension/README.md) shows that independently fitted value-quartile isotonic calibration matches or exceeds the logistic graph certification counts; interpret the earlier alignment result through this conditional-calibration finding.

The [alignment and repeated-vendor extension](../revision_complete/alignment_extension/README.md) adds twenty consequence permutations, ten vendor splits, and PO-grouped policy/selection contrasts. It supersedes any general cold-start conclusion based on one vendor allocation.

The grouped extension adds [PO/vendor holdouts and component audits](../revision_complete/grouped_component_extension/README.md). Its cluster intervals supersede case-only intervals for interpreted component effects; original outputs remain available. Run the grouped extension after the full-population study.

The current study evaluates component tradeoffs on all 210,692 eligible natural
cases rather than the capped intervention-source universe. Read the
[full protocol and reproduction instructions](../revision_complete/robustness_extension/README.md).

The main changes are independent fixed-sequence selection, an independently
learned starting-point variant, multi-chain RCSE testing, 20 calibration-half
repetitions, an earlier fixed-90-day temporal refit, a nonlinear safety comparator,
exact cost break-even curves, and log/linear consequence sensitivity.

The headline discrimination quantity is AUROC **within complete-evidence cases**.
Overall discrimination partly includes an observable GR-missing rule already
present in the outcome definition. Full-population IID complete-evidence AUROC is
0.7694 for logistic regression and 0.8554 for histogram gradient boosting. That
nonlinear advantage is not established in the earlier temporal or exposure-OOD
comparisons, and the nonlinear model degrades sharply in the later temporal test.

At the first calibration-half seed, the logistic reference RCSE rule and gate plus
safety at 0.95 select exactly the same IID and earlier-temporal cases. Stronger
safety-threshold selection can certify at a 2% target; a blanket claim that safety
thresholds select nothing is unsupported across procedures and calibration splits.
Certification frequency is not a deployment or distribution-shift guarantee.

Mean modeled loss depends on the specified consequence function and human cost.
The cost outputs provide exact break-even values for fixed decisions, and separate
curves for recomputed RCSE actions. Raw PO-value-weighted UER is included but does
not measure actual financial harm. Interpret incomplete outcomes through the
saved ambiguity bounds.

Earlier experiment directories retain historical protocols and diagnostic results.
They use different cohorts or model calibration and should not be merged into a
single headline comparison. Manuscripts, reviewer correspondence, editing helpers,
and editorial logs remain excluded from the public research repository.

PO-grouped complete-evidence AUROCs are 0.7406 and 0.8026; vendor-grouped AUROCs are 0.8052 and 0.8057. The logistic linear temporal RCSE-minus-safety interval includes zero; the boosted linear temporal contrast is negative with its cluster interval below zero. Absolute exposure totals and concentration sensitivities are included.
